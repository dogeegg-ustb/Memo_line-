# Open-source dependencies

- ag-psd 30.2.0 — MIT — https://github.com/Agamnentzar/ag-psd — PSD editing/writing library.
- @webtoon/psd 0.4.0 — MIT — https://github.com/webtoon/psd — independent, zero-dependency PSD reader.
- pngjs 7.0.0 — MIT — https://github.com/pngjs/pngjs — lossless PNG I/O.
- base64-js 1.5.1 — MIT — https://github.com/beatgammit/base64-js — dependency of ag-psd.
- pako 2.1.0 — MIT AND Zlib — https://github.com/nodeca/pako — dependency of ag-psd.

Full upstream licenses are retained under backend/node_modules in the published program. Node.js and .NET runtimes are bundled by Build.ps1; their licenses are included in the published distribution.
